    \item A spur gearset has a pitch of 4 teeth/in and a speed ratio of 1/4. The pinion has 16 teeth.
    Find the number of teeth on the gear, the pitch diameters, and the theoretical center-to-
    center distance.
    \\
    Solution: center-to-center-distance is between 6 – 12 in
    \\~\\
    Center-tocenter distance can be expressed as: $\frac{d_{pinion}+d_{gear}}{2}$
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Diametral Pitch $\equiv P =\frac{N}{d} =[\frac{teeth}{in}]$ 
        \begin{itemize}
            \item $P_{gear}=4$
            \item $P_{pinion}=?$ by rule 2: $P_{gear}=P_{pinion}$
            \item $N_{pinion} = 16$
        \end{itemize}
        \item Speed Ratio $\equiv S_{ratio} = \frac{n_{out}}{n_{in}} = \frac{d_{pinion}}{d_{gear}}=1/4$
    \end{itemize}
    diameter of pinion can be expressed as: $d_{pinion} = d_{gear}/4$ (I)
    \\
    by rule 2: $P_{gear}=\frac{N_{pinion}}{d_{pinion}}$ (II)
    \\
    combining eq. (I) and (II):
    \begin{align*}
        \frac{N_{pinion}}{P_{gear}}&=d_{gear}/4
        \\
        d_{gear} &= 4(16/4) = 16 in
    \end{align*}
    Speed ratio can be used to calculate $d_{pinion}$: $d_{pinion}=16/4=4in$
    \\~\\
    \textbf{Answer: Center-to-center distance $= \frac{d_{pinion}+d_{gear}}{2} = \frac{20}{2} = 10in$}
