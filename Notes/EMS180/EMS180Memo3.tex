\noindent This document summarizes the the results of a fact-finding research on the three sustainability capitals (People, Planet, and Prosperity) of Wind Farms. 
\\~\\
To begin the investigationThe current growth average forecasted from 2025 to 2030 is 8 \% per year according to \href{https://www.gwec.net/gwec-news/wind-industry-installs-record-capacity-in-2024-despite-policy-instability}{[1]}. 
This is notably less than the growth rate number from Ashby's chapter on Wind Farms which was 25 \% per year.
