\section{Final presentation material index}
Objective - Minimize deflection $\delta$: $$\delta =\frac{F_fL^3}{C_1EI}$$
\\~\\
Since $F_f$, $L$, and $C_1$ are constants, $\delta \propto EI$
\\~\\
\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.47\textwidth}
\centering
\DrawSolidBox{0.9}

\smallskip
$A_s$ (solid square)
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.47\textwidth}
\centering
\DrawHollowBox{0.9}{0.55}

\smallskip
$A_h$ (hollow square)
\end{minipage}
\end{center}

For the solid square section ($A_s$) with side \(b\):
\[
I_s=\frac{b^{4}}{12},\qquad
\text{and since } A_s=b^{2},\quad
I_s=\frac{A_s^{2}}{12}.
\]

For the hollow square section ($A_h$) with outer side \(b\) and inner side \(\gamma b\):
\[
I_h=\frac{b^{4}-\gamma^{4}b^{4}}{12}
=\frac{(1-\gamma^{4})\,b^{4}}{12}.
\]
Using the solid reference area \(A_s=b^{2}\),
\[
I_h=\frac{(1-\gamma^{4})\,A_s^{2}}{12}.
\]

Introduce the bending shape factor \(\phi\) (normalized so a solid square gives \(\phi=1\)):
\[
\phi=\frac{12\,I}{A_s^{\,2}}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\begin{cases}
\phi_s=\dfrac{12\,I_s}{A_s^{2}}=1, \\[1.0ex]
\phi_h=\dfrac{12\,I_h}{A_s^{2}}=1-\gamma^{4}.
\end{cases}
\]

Thus,
\[
\boxed{\ \phi=\dfrac{12\,I}{A_s^{2}}\ }
\]
encapsulates bending efficiency; it equals \(1\) for the solid square and decreases with hollowness for fixed reference area \(A_s\).
\\~\\
Mass as a constraint:
\begin{center}
    \includegraphics[width=200pt]{Screenshot 2025-12-01 170823.png}
\end{center}
From $m = \rho V$:
$$b^4=\bigg[\frac{m}{\rho (1-\gamma^2)L}\bigg]^2$$
expanding Inertia term:
\begin{align*}
    I &= \frac{b^4}{12}-\frac{\gamma^4 b^4}{12}
    \\
    I &= \frac{(1-\gamma^4)}{12}b^4
\end{align*}
combining inertia and mass equations:
$$I = \frac{(1-\gamma^4)}{12(1-\gamma^2)^2}\frac{m^2}{\rho^2L^2}$$
Plug inertia term back into objective function:
$$\delta =\frac{F_fL^3}{C_1E}\frac{12(1-\gamma^2)^2}{(1-\gamma^4)}\frac{\rho^2L^2}{m^2} = \frac{F_fL^3}{C_1}\frac{12(1-\gamma^2)^2}{(1-\gamma^4)}\frac{L^2}{m^2}\bigg(\frac{\rho^2}{E}\bigg)$$
\newpage
material index M:
\begin{align*}
    M &= \frac{\rho^2}{E}
    \\
    M_h &= \frac{(1-\gamma^2)^2}{(1-\gamma^4)}\frac{\rho^2}{E}
    \\
    M_h &= \phi\frac{\rho^2}{E}
    \\
    \log M &= 2\log \rho - \log E
    \\
    \log E &= 2\log \rho - \log M
\end{align*}
