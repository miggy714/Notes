\section{Final presentation material index}
Objective - Minimize deflection $\delta$: $$\delta =\frac{F_fL^3}{C_1EI}$$
\\~\\
Constraints - Failure force $F_f$: $$F_f = Z\frac{\sigma_y}{L}$$
\\~\\
Since $F_f$, $L$, and $C_1$ are constants, $\delta \propto EI$
\\~\\
Recover density term from Inertia:

\begin{center}
    \includegraphics[width=200pt]{Screenshot 2025-12-01 170823.png}
\end{center}
From $m = \rho V$:
$$b^4=\bigg[\frac{m}{\rho (1-\gamma^2)L}\bigg]^2$$
expanding Inertia term:
\begin{align*}
    I &= \frac{b^4}{12}-\frac{\gamma^4 b^4}{12}
    \\
    I &= \frac{(1-\gamma)^4}{12}b^4
\end{align*}
combining inertia and mass equations:
$$I = \frac{(1-\gamma)^4}{12(1-\gamma^2)^2}\frac{m^2}{\rho^2L^2}$$
Plug inertia term back into objective function:
$$\delta =\frac{F_fL^3}{C_1E}\frac{12(1-\gamma^2)^2}{(1-\gamma)^4}\frac{\rho^2L^2}{m^2} = \frac{F_fL^3}{C_1}\frac{12(1-\gamma^2)^2}{(1-\gamma)^4}\frac{L^2}{m^2}\bigg(\frac{\rho^2}{E}\bigg)$$
